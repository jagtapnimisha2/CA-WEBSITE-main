GST Return Filing

GST refers to Goods and Services Tax, which came into force on 1st July 2017, and is an indirect tax imposed on the exchange of goods and services between organizations. GST returns must be filed at each step of the production and distribution process. The main motto of GST is One Nation One Tax. Proper and timely GST return filing ensures compliance with tax regulations, helps in accurate tax reporting, and avoids penalties. [CA] offers expert services to manage the entire GST return filing process, ensuring that your business adheres to all statutory requirements effortlessly.

TDS Return Filing

The acronym TDS stands for Tax Deducted at Source, a tax levied by the government at the time of a transaction. The tax amount is deducted either when the money is credited to the payee's account or when the payment is made, whichever occurs first. Filing TDS returns involves submitting details of these tax deductions to the government periodically. Accurate TDS return filing ensures compliance with tax regulations, helps in maintaining proper financial records, and avoids penalties. [CA] provides comprehensive services for managing TDS return filing, ensuring that your business meets all legal requirements efficiently and on time.

Professional Tax Registration

Professional Tax is a tax levied by the State Government for the infrastructure it provides to citizens, enabling them to carry out their professions smoothly. This tax applies to various professions, trades, and employments, with its applicability based on the income of the individuals or entities involved. Registering for Professional Tax is mandatory for businesses and professionals, ensuring compliance with state laws and contributing to the state's infrastructure development. [CA] offers expert services for Professional Tax registration, handling all necessary procedures to ensure seamless compliance for your profession or business.

Income Tax Return Filing

For income earned in the Financial Year 2022-23, the last date to file an Income Tax Return (ITR) is 31st July. The new Assessment Year 2023-24 began on April 1. Typically, the due date for filing an ITR is July 31, and it is expected to remain the same this year. Although the government has extended the filing deadlines in the past for various reasons, it is anticipated that there will be no extension this year. This expectation arises because the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) has notified the new ITR forms for AY 2023-24 more than a month in advance. Timely filing of ITR ensures compliance with tax regulations and avoids penalties.
