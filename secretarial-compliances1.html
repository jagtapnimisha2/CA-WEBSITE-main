Annual Compliances for Private Limited Company

Compliance refers to the ability to adhere to directives, guidelines, or regulations. A private limited company established in India must ensure that it meets all the requirements stipulated by the Companies Act of 2013. Adhering to these regulations involves fulfilling various annual compliance obligations, which are essential for the smooth and lawful operation of the company.

Annual Filing for LLP

A Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) is a distinct legal entity registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) in India. To register an LLP, there must be at least two partners, one of whom must be an Indian citizen and resident. The partners of an LLP are responsible for maintaining proper books of accounts, filing Income Tax Returns, and submitting an Annual Return with the MCA every financial year. Ensuring timely and accurate annual filings is crucial for compliance and the smooth operation of the LLP.

MCA Compliance

Regulatory compliance, also known as corporate compliance, refers to an organization's adherence to all relevant rules and regulations governing its activities and industry. These regulations may be imposed by local, state, or federal governments. Compliance with MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) regulations is essential for the proper functioning of any organization. AGR-Return Filing

Adjusted Gross Revenue (AGR) is a type of tax levied on telecom operators based on two parameters: a basic charge for usage and a licensing fee for providing telecom services in the country. The submission of these two statements by telecom operators to the Controller of Communication of Accounts (CCA) is known as AGR-Return filing. Any telecom license holder, who has obtained the license under Section 4 of the National Telegraph Act, must submit the AGR statements to the CCA of the Department of Telecommunications.

Form AOC-4

The evaluation of a company's performance by its Board of Directors and shareholders often involves referencing the company's financial statements. These financial statements must be submitted to the Registrar of Companies using Form AOC-4 for each fiscal year. The company is required to submit Form AOC-4 within 30 days of its completion. Under the Companies Act of 2013, all Indian registered companies are obligated to file Form AOC-4 annually. Failure to complete or delays in submitting the required documentation may result in fines or penalties for the company.

Form MGT-7

Every company is mandated to furnish the Registrar of Companies with an annual return. This annual return is prepared using Form MGT-7. The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) necessitates that all companies submit their annual return information via the digitized Form MGT-7. This requirement is applicable to all companies
